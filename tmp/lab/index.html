Landscape full-screen lab

Open in Safari on the iPhone. Each page says in big type whether Safari's bars are hidden, and logs what hid or brought them back. Every reading is also sent to the Mac by itself, so there is nothing to copy.

A new page always starts with the bars showing. Open, turn the phone to landscape, follow the grey line.

  1. V0 · Still
    Today's site: the page never scrolls, the row is driven by touch. Baseline: bars should never hide in landscape.
  2. V1 · Swipe up, then lock
    Tall invisible page under a fixed stage. One upward swipe hides the bars, then the page locks. The mechanism to ship. Also: lock with overflow:hidden · no lock.
  3. V4 · Candidate
    The full plan: rotate prompt upright, landscape stage, first swipe hides the bars and moves the row. Clean, no numbers.
  4. V2 · Page scroll drives the row
    Every vertical swipe scrolls the page and moves the row. Do bars come back when you go back?
  5. V3 · Fullscreen API
    Element fullscreen, video fullscreen, orientation lock: what the iPhone answers.
  6. V5 · Sideways page scroll
    Does a native horizontal page scroll hide the bars?
  7. V6 · Sideways scroll box
    Does a native overflow-x row hide the bars?
  8. V7 · Vertical scroll box
    A scroll box (not the page) drives the row. iOS 15+ collapses bars for boxes too.
  9. V8 · In-app browsers
    Open from LinkedIn / Instagram / Gmail: does it rotate, does “Open in Safari” work?
  10. V9 · Home Screen app
    Add to Home Screen: what a web app gets (not realistic for a recruiter, for reference).

Readout: green = bars hidden (or minimised, upright). “page 393 of 393 px tall” = fills the screen. Blue hairline = the page's viewport; coloured ticks at the right = 100svh / 100lvh / 100dvh (a missing letter = that unit runs past the bottom of the screen). Tap the big line to fold the numbers away.
