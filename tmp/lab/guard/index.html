Safari guard lab

Open each on the iPhone, in Safari first, then from LinkedIn / Instagram / Gmail (send yourself the link). Landscape first.

  1. G1 · Full guard

    The recommended set on a landscape stage. Try every way to break it (two-thumb pinch, double-tap, left-edge swipe with the photo open and closed, pull down, long-press, drag the photo out, rotate). The readout shows the zoom, history, bars, the site’s own pinch and the last thing the guard blocked. Copy report and paste it in the chat. The layers row turns one guard off at a time: e.g. turn off touch-action and 2-finger to see whether gesture alone still stops the page zoom; turn on edge pd to see that a touchstart preventDefault does not stop the back swipe.

  2. G2 · Unguarded control

    The same stage, no guard. Do the same things: Safari zooms, the photo pinch is cancelled, the edge swipe leaves, the top pull refreshes, long-press shows the callout.

  3. G3 · Load zoomed

    Pinch in, then reload; then go to page 2 and swipe back; then rotate while zoomed. It shows the scale the page comes back at, and three recoveries: A meta reset (expect: no effect in Safari, works in LinkedIn / Instagram), B same-URL replace and B2 new-URL load (expect ×1), C counter-zoom (the stage looks un-zoomed); auto runs B at load when zoomed. G3 with the guard: you cannot zoom in at all.

The real verdict is the phone’s: the Mac’s WebKit has no Safari gestures. Source: resp/guard-src/ · notes/safari-guards.md
